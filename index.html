💬 Voice Cheat Sheet
Say: "[Product] [size] [cans/bottles] need [number]"

Examples:
• "Pepsi 12 pack cans need 8"
• "Diet Pepsi 1.25 liter bottles need 6"
• "Mountain Dew 20 oz bottles need 10"
• "Gatorade 24 pack need 3"
• "Aquafina 20 oz need 12"
• "Pepsi Zero 12 pack cans need 4"
• "Bubly 12 pack cans need 2"

You can say multiple in one go:
"Pepsi 12 pack cans need 8, Diet Pepsi 2 liter need 4"
